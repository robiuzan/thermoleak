## Dropped

- **"לנזילה פעילה נשתדל להגיע באותו יום"** (answer, meta, travel): removed. The same-day radius is unconfirmed (docs/business-facts.md §E), and neither check could verify it. The owner may restore it.
- **"רחובות רבים בה עולים ויורדים במדרון"**: no source found. Replaced by the city engineer's "עיר טופוגרפית".
- **1960s–70s four-flat buildings as a citywide layer**: the source covers one compound. Now stated only for the Bereshit compound.
- **Any distance to central Tel Aviv ("פחות מ-5 ק״מ", then "כ-5 ק״מ")**: no published source states one. From en.wiki's coordinates (Givatayim 32°04′17″N 34°48′36″E; Rothschild Blvd 32°3′55.87″N 34°46′37.3″E) the straight line is about 3.2 km; "about 5 km" is only a road estimate, also behind the 5 km in docs/business-facts.md §E. The page says only that the two cities share a border, which is sourced.
- **TAMA 38 totals for 2005–2020** (34 strengthening, 77 rebuild, 111 in all): one secondary source with no named data source. Replaced by "רבים".
- **The March 2021 freeze of urban renewal** ("במרץ 2021 הקפיאה עיריית גבעתיים את ההתחדשות העירונית בתחומה", nadlancenter 4316): confirmed, but cut for length.
- **"כל הדיירים חולקים גג אחד" (Bereshit)**: wrong for garden-roof four-flat buildings, where the roof sits over the upper flats.
- **Bereshit unit counts** (about 115 units in 2023, 186 units in 2024): the two counts differ, probably because the compound's boundary changed. Not stated.
- **"The pipes inside the building were not replaced in the works"**: no primary text was found that sets where the water corporation's network ends. Reworded to say only that the street works tell nothing about the pipes inside.
- **"The thermal scan maps the source" / "a finding, not a guess"**: overclaims against the site's rule that a thermal finding alone is not proof. Replaced by "narrows the search; a moisture meter verifies".
- **FAQ on the floor that used to be the top after a TAMA 38 addition**: it would read the same on other cities' pages, so it failed the substitution test. Replaced by the Mei Givatayim FAQ.
- **The north-west plan as still pending**: out of date. The 2026 policy document approval was added.
- **Exact height of Givat Kozlovsky (85 m / 87 m) and "the highest point in Gush Dan"**: the sources conflict. Replaced by "more than 80 m" and "among the highest".
- **Borochov as "the first workers' neighbourhood in the country"**: he.wiki only, with no footnote.
- **Founding years of Kiryat Yosef, Sheinkin, Givat Rambam and Arlozorov**: the sources conflict (1933 vs 1934; 1925 vs 1936). Folded into "through the 1930s".
- **The Givat Kozlovsky water reservoir and the 2021–2023 storage shortage**: the operator conflicts between sources, the current status was not found, and there is no honest link to leaks inside a home.
- **"Third densest city"**: no citation.
- **The north-west towers (32–45 floors) and the Katzenelson tower**: plans, not built, and not about leaks.
- **Katzenelson Street flooding**: rests on one resident's undated quote.
- **The Mei Givatayim 2023 budget**: a shekel amount, not needed.
