## Dropped

- **"Route 4 divides two worlds: the old east and the new west."** Refuted. Ramat Eliyahu, west of Route 4, dates from 1936 (S1, S10). The first dune neighbourhoods were built in the mid-1970s (S3). Narkisim, east of Route 4, is new.
- **"Pipe age, and even the soil under the building, change with the side of the road."** Refuted. There are old and new buildings on both sides. The dunes historically reached about as far east as Route 412 (S3: "גבולו המזרחי של האזור חופף בערך לתוואי של כביש 412").
- **A citywide "1970s–80s apartment blocks".** It came from a style description of one neighbourhood (S4). It is replaced by S1's 1960s–70s apartment blocks.
- **Katznelson as "low, 3–4 floors" and "between Jabotinsky and Route 4" without the Begin boundary.** The sources disagree on height. S4 describes only the part south of Begin.
- **"The original pipes are about four to five decades old."** No source dates the construction. S4 also reports that strengthening-and-extension projects dominate in Katznelson, so the original pipes cannot be assumed.
- **Narkisim "on land being vacated."** The land has already been vacated (S1, S14).
- **Electrical-panel FAQ for the מב"ת districts (מב"ת צפון, מב"ת מערב).** Dropped to keep the location pages distinct: another location page already answers this question. Its line "a fault heats up only under load" also overstated the physics, since the heat rises with the current rather than switching on.
- **Landmark claim: the Great Synagogue (1885–1889), the 1898 water tower and the well "מצאנו מים".** Dropped as a claim because these are landmarks, not housing. The synagogue and the tower remain only as orientation in intro[0]. The well's date (completed 23 February 1883, per he.wikipedia "מצאנו מים (באר)") is not used.
- **Ramat Eliyahu's founding year.** End of 1936 (S1, S10) vs 1945 (a photo caption in S1).
- **The renewal plan's approval year.** Sources give 2021 or 2022.
- **The height of Ramat Eliyahu's railway blocks.** 3–4 floors (S7) vs 2–3 (S8, S9).
- **Distance from Tel Aviv (~20 km).** No source. S2's "eight kilometres south of Tel Aviv" is ambiguous. The page names roads only.
- **"39% of residents live west of Route 4."** The only source was a ticketing site, with no statistical table behind it.
- **Local flooding history.** The only reports were single and oddly dated, and none could be confirmed.
- **Drainage-network figures** (about 300 km of drainage pipe, infiltration lakes). True, but they gave no honest point about leak detection.
- **Carmel winery.** The sources conflict on its founding and closing dates, and it is not housing.
- **"Fourth" or "fifth" largest city.** The sources disagree, and it is a superlative.
- **Nahalat Yehuda and Ramez renewal figures.** Verified, but renewal is kept to one claim.
- **The Shafdan works on the western dunes; the city conservation plan רצ/2030.** No leak-detection relevance.
- **A new-build FAQ under the Sale (Apartments) Law.** It is a legal statement, and the secondary sources disagree on the defect periods.
- **Neot Shikma** (built in the late 1990s on the dunes). Verified, but cut for length.
