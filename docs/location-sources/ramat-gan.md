## Dropped

- **נווה יהושע (founded 1944–1947 for discharged soldiers; towers discussed in December 2025).** The only source contradicts itself: it also says founded 1946, first residents summer 1947. Only one of the two December 2025 plans is confirmed approved for deposit, and the claim's leak angle repeated another claim on this page.
- **"שדרות ירושלים" among the November 2020 flooded streets.** Mako lists it under רמת גן, but the blind check found no other report and a better-known 2020 flood on Jaffa's street of the same name. The page names no street.
- **"Most of רמת עמידר's buildings are 1950s railway blocks, 65–75 years old."** Bizportal says so. Wikipedia says the original houses were two-storey and that blocks of flats were added from the 1970s. The page says "some" and gives no age range.
- **An exact founding year for רמת עמידר** (1949, 1950 or "early 1950s"). The sources differ, so the page says "the state's first years".
- **"כ-6 ק״מ ממרכז תל אביב".** No citable source supports it. The straight-line distance is about 3 km, and the cities share a border.
- **Tower heights of "55 and 60 storeys".** These are right for the May 2025 approval, but later reports give 55–61, so the page says "more than 50".
- **"Every TAMA 38 building holds two construction generations".** This is true only for strengthening plus added floors (38/1). Most of רמת גן's TAMA 38 output was demolish-and-rebuild (38/2), per Madlan.
- **"Without stopping work" for panel scans.** The site promises no power cut, not no disruption to a business.
- **"The scan shows the water's path" and "the scan is clearer after rain".** Both claim more than thermal imaging can do, and neither appears in the site's own method copy.
- **פתח תקווה as a nearby area.** It does not border רמת גן: בני ברק and גבעת שמואל lie between them.
- **The 2005–2020 urban-renewal totals, Madlan's "about 18%" share, and "75% of 2016 sales were TAMA 38".** These came second-hand, undated or unsourced.
- **The 2019 municipal TAMA 38 freeze and the court rulings that followed.** The dates and the "about 200 stalled projects" figure were not confirmed, and the topic is legal.
- **The שיכון ותיקים and רמת חן flooding histories.** Their wording looks copied between sites, there are no independent reports, and one of the soil descriptions belongs to a different site.
- **The heights of מגדל משה אביב and other towers.** Sources conflict, and the rankings are out of date.
- **"מהערים המובילות" (among the leading cities).** This is a superlative. The page uses the dated, attributed Calcalist ranking instead.
