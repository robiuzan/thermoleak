## Dropped

- **"20–25 km from central Tel Aviv"**: no source measures from the city centre, and 25 km appears only on de.wikipedia. Now "about 20 km from Tel Aviv".
- **"The heritage survey was carried out in 2007"**: 2007 is only the date of the ynet report (19.06.07). The article does not date the survey itself, so the page gives no year.
- **"The Yarkon–Negev line was inaugurated in 1955"**: in 1955 only the eastern line opened, and the full line was completed in 1961. Now scoped to the eastern line.
- **">5,000 flats" attributed to Pisgot Afek**: Bizportal's 5,000 is the city-wide count of "מחיר למשתכן" flats. Pisgot Afek holds "most" of them.
- **"The thermal scan locates the source"**: it claims more than thermal imaging can do alone. Replaced by "narrows the search, and a moisture meter verifies it".
- **"We do not commit to a same-day visit"**: an operating rule with no public or owner-confirmed source. Only "by prior arrangement" is kept.
- **"The springs lie between Petah Tikva and Rosh HaAyin"**: the source places Tel Afek's road entrance there, not the springs. Replaced by "at the foot of Tel Afek, within Yarkon National Park".
- **Sale (Apartments) Law periods (4-year inspection, then 3-year warranty)**: true per the law's text, but legal statements are kept off this page.
- **Hill-slope claim (lower-floor walls touching the soil)**: general building physics, not local data.
- **Under-floor leak question for a Givat Tal house**: nothing in it was specific to the neighbourhood, and Givat Tal's own construction year is not sourced.
- **Elevations of about 50 m (west) and about 200 m (east)**: found only on Wikipedia and its mirror. Not independently checked.
- **Pisgot Afek planned totals (14,000 / 15,500 / 15,700, or 13,500)**: the sources conflict, and 15,700 is the citywide framework agreement.
- **Pisgot Afek occupancy start (2017)**: single source, and a 2016 Knesset motion implies earlier completions.
- **Water corporation "עין אפק"**: its official site could not be read, and no leak-relevant local fact was found.
- **Afek industrial park (1990s)**: the founding sentence has no footnote.
- **Nitzba neighbourhood (2019–2022)**: a single unfootnoted sentence, with no second source.
- **"The city is built on limestone"**: Wikipedia only, and any link to leaks would be speculative.
- **The British camp's silicate-brick buildings**: heritage structures, not housing.
- **2025 master plan (+≈15,700 units, renewal along Shabazi)**: future plans, not current housing stock.
- **The 1990s eastern neighbourhoods were built for career-soldier associations**: true per he.wikipedia, but it has no bearing on leaks.
- **Population 81,884 (August 2026)**: the CBS monthly table could not be opened. The 2021 estimate is used instead.
- **"Population tripled 1983–2005"**: the CBS census has 37,900 in 2008. Census figures are used instead.
- **"About 35,000 immigrants passed through the ma'abara"**: Wikipedia itself hedges it.
- **Green Line about 1.5 km away**: not relevant to leaks.
