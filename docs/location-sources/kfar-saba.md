## Dropped

- **The water-bill leak-relief procedure** (150% threshold, six-month deadline, online form with a declaration of repair, credit at the leak tariff, the 24-hour remote-reading rule). These terms follow the national Water Authority rules, so they are not specific to Kfar Saba.
- **The Yoseftal renewal step.** On 28.1.2026 the local committee gave in-principle approval to keep advancing the renewal plan. It is a preliminary planning stage and does not help anyone find a leak today.
- **"Via נתיבי איילון" and a single 23 km figure.** The direct routes use Ayalon for 2 km at most, and the distance depends on the starting point.
- **"The report is handed over at the end of the visit".** The report turnaround is not confirmed.
- **The number of floors the renewal plan allows** (3, or 4 on active streets, in the 2023 version of the plan; one floor plus a partial floor in the 2025 track). The figure changed between plan stages.
- **Built-in planters on "sun balconies" under the 1976 plan.** The only source is one uncited Wikipedia sentence.
- **Green roofs mandatory on new buildings since 2014.** The municipal page cited for it states no obligation.
- **"Kfar Saba has no municipal water corporation" as a distinction.** No source says so.
- **Water sources** (wells, reservoirs, a water tower). They have no honest link to leaks in buildings.
- **The number of housing units in Yoseftal.** The sources disagree: 1,051, 955 and 930–932.
- **Population.** The figures conflict, and none is needed.
- **"About 40 km from Tel Aviv"** (drushim.co.il). The routing results refute it.
- **Older neighbourhoods** (שבזי 1944, קפלן's 28 m² houses, שכונות הפועלים 1933–1935, תקומה). Each rests on a single source, or nothing shows what still stands.
- **The 2008 green-building decision.** The only source is an undated transcript, and the decision is about energy, not leaks.
- **The governance of מפעל המים** (an appointed committee and a pending petition). It is still changing, and it is no use to a homeowner.
