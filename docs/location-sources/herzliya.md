## Dropped

- **"About 14 km north of central Tel Aviv."** No public source gives 14 km. The page uses he.wikipedia's "about 10 km north of Tel Aviv-Yafo".
- **Height of the kurkar ridge ("25–30 m").** The sources disagree. MUNI-2021 gives 25–30 m, a 2025 report (kikar.co.il, 16.01.25) puts a Herzliya cliff at about 35 m, and the Sharon Escarpment reaches 45 m. The page gives no height.
- **"Sea-facing walls next to the ridge are directly exposed to storms."** Most of הרצליה פיתוח lies on the plain behind the coastal ridge, which gives it partial shelter. The page speaks instead of wind-driven rain on west-facing walls, with a hedge.
- **Average annual rainfall "about 540 mm, half in December–January".** The 540 mm figure appears only in MUNI-2021. IMS publishes no Herzliya normal. Its 1991–2020 averages for the neighbouring stations are 534 mm (Kefar Shmaryahu) and 557 mm (Hakfar Hayarok), and its November 2020 summary implies a lower average at the Herzliya station. The claim is not used, because the timing point is not specific to Herzliya.
- **"About 1,400 flats" in יד התשעה.** Superseded by the April 2026 count of 1,483 (kept as about 1,500).
- **Ayalon "extended to the Seven Stars interchange".** True for 2000, but the road has run further north since 2017. The page keeps only "reached the city in 2000".
- **Irrigation lines in the yard.** Not a service the site offers. The FAQ is limited to the private supply line.
- **26/11/2020 as an "over 1:100-year" event, and "about 70% of 108 flood claims in 2020".** Stated only in MUNI-2021, and not needed.
- **The April 2026 יד התשעה renewal plan (1,483 → 4,616 units, approved for deposit 15.04.26).** Verified (nadlancenter.co.il/article/14311) but not used. It does not bear on leak detection in the existing buildings.
- **About 94 "railway buildings" in יד התשעה.** No source found.
- **"Prefab shikunim from the early 1960s" in יד התשעה.** The cited source does not support it.
- **How many flats were actually expanded under the 2001 plan.** No source found, so the seam sentence is written as a condition.
- **נווה עמל lot sizes as numbers.** The sources conflict (400–750 m² against 500 m² to a dunam).
