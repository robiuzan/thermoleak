# מודיעין — sources for /locations/modiin/

Researched and checked on 2026-10-28. Each statement below was checked twice: once against the live
source pages (source-fidelity check), and once in an independent blind check that tried to refute
it. Quotes were taken from the live pages that day, so spot-check the exact wording before you rely
on it. **Every claim still needs the owner's confirmation** before it is treated as settled. The
checks show that public sources support the claims. They do not replace the knowledge of someone

## Dropped

- **D1. Re'ut "founded in the 1980s".** Sources give 1987, 1988, after the 1990 approval, and the early 1990s. Replaced with "a few years after Maccabim".
- **D2. "The neighbourhoods sit on the hills themselves".** No live source says this, and Safdie and Globes say slopes and hillsides. Replaced.
- **D3. "Low-rise climbing from the valley to the hilltop", and the four-storey rule applied to every building today.** Safdie puts larger, denser structures on the hilltops, and the city as built also has dense and taller buildings. The plan is described as a plan, and the advice is limited to low buildings.
- **D4. "Outside Gush Dan".** he.wiki's גוש דן article lists מודיעין-מכבים-רעות in the metropolitan area's outer ring. Removed.
- **D5. The new-flat "contractor's בדק period" line and the Sale (Apartments) Law FAQ.** The law makes the seller (המוכר) liable, not the contractor, and the sources disagree on how long the בדק period is for waterproofing. The page now carries no legal statement.
- **D6. "In a new flat, a leak is more likely to come from the workmanship than from wear".** This is reasoning, not a sourced statistic. Removed.
- **D7. Mei Modiin's repair and relief deadlines.** The leak page says 30 days, the relief form says six months from the end of the billing period. The two disagree, so only "relief after repair" is kept.
- **D8. "In a detached house, all possible sources are inside the property".** Contradicted by shared walls and by moisture coming from the soil. That FAQ was replaced.
- **D9. נופים "still going up" and נופים's occupancy year.** No source says it is still under construction. he.wiki says occupied from 2019, but a January 2018 report already described it as being populated. No year is given.
- **D10. First occupancy "July 1996".** No source gives a month.
- **D11. A construction-start year.** he.wiki says 1994, en.wiki says the cornerstone was laid in 1993. Ambiguous, so not used.
- **D12. Mandatory stone cladding.** No planning or municipal source was found.
- **D13. המעיינות (3,575 units; towers of 19 or 25 storeys; plan approved 19/10/2026).** Still a plan, so it is not described as housing stock. If construction starts, recheck whether מורשת and נופים are still the youngest neighbourhoods.
- **D14. הציפורים unit counts and dates.** The sources differ slightly, and the figures are not needed.
- **D15. Mei Modiin leak-alert app.** The source URL now returns 404. Unverified.
- **D16. "50% of the valleys are public parks", the railway dates, the population figures.** True or close to it, but not relevant to leaks. The population figures also disagree between sources.
- **D17. Distance "~40 km".** No source gives it. The sourced figure is about 35 km.
