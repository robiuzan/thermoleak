## Dropped

- **Ramat Verber plan "deposited in 2020".** 2020 was only approval for deposit; the formal deposit came in December 2021.
- **Ramat Verber plan "adds 3,600 units".** That was the May 2020 figure; the deposited plan proposes about 3,945. "Adds" also overstated a framework plan.
- **Ramat Verber "buildings of up to 32 storeys".** That was the 2020 limit for the Jabotinsky axis only. Detailed plans have since reached 33 storeys (article 14599, 27.05.26) and up to 35 (article 10369, 05.08.24). No height is given.
- **Ramat Verber plan "since approved".** Reported approval dates conflict (December 2024 in article 13690; about a year earlier in article 9862), and one check found no approval. Not claimed.
- **Ramat Verber "built in the early 1950s as rows of three-storey buildings".** This merged two stocks. The early-1950s rows are two-family buildings, and the three-storey "rail buildings" carry no date in the source.
- **Ramat Verber "in the city centre".** The sources disagree: he-wiki's neighbourhood article says centre, but he-wiki's city article and nadlancenter 14599 say west. Replaced with its bounding streets.
- **"Ramat Verber is being replanned as a tower neighbourhood".** The plan puts towers only along the axes, and strengthens existing buildings and keeps the park in the centre.
- **Kfar Ganim "three generations of building stand" and "founded 1926".** Almost none of the original buildings remain. The name covers four sub-areas, including בת גנים (1990s). The founding year is given only as a decade (see claims[3]).
- **Pinsker Street: a 25-storey tower with a Bauhaus and an eclectic building kept (חצר שפירא, given validity by the district committee in 2026).** Confirmed, but it rests on a single trade-press article and adds no leak-specific point. Left out.
- **Single-family houses in Kfar Ganim C (he-wiki).** Confirmed but not used. The roof-or-pipe question it supported is not specific to this city.
