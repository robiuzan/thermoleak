## Dropped

- **Kiryat Sharet "founded in the 1950s for immigrants, still largely three-storey railway buildings".** Refuted: Calcalist says it is mainly private houses, and demolition of the Geula railway buildings began in July 2023. No source was found for the founding story.
- **Neve Zemer "built since the middle of the last decade".** Refuted: work began in July 2012 (nadlancenter/8091).
- **Neve Zemer "still under construction" in 2026.** Only 2023 articles and search-index text support it. The page now gives only the start of work and the expected total.
- **"Six-storey buildings in some Neve Zemer projects".** Only one project (Vilnaski Street) is sourced.
- **Kiryat David Remez's 3–6-storey buildings called "low", and widened to all the old neighbourhoods.** The source says neither.
- **About 400 buildings / about 4,000 apartments under the plan.** These belong to the 2023 deposit version. The figures for the plan in force since December 2024 were not found, and the approved plan is reported with a different number (3,770–4,000 new units).
- **Exact well count, the 46% / 54% split, and "a large share" from wells.** The corporation's own pages disagree (8, 7 and 6 wells).
- **"The city's two water towers" and "the city's symbol" as plain fact.** At least three historic towers stand. Kept only as the corporation's own statement.
- **Area of Kiryat Etgarim and of the junction office parks.** Sources conflict (900 vs 470 dunams; 120 vs 55 dunams).
- **Deep groundwater, so damp at the base of a wall is rarely groundwater.** Today's water-table depth could not be sourced.
- **Share of private houses as a percentage.** No CBS or municipal figure was found.
- **An unconditional "the thermal scan can locate the source".** It conflicts with the site's stated limits for inactive or deep leaks.
- **The founding company "אחוזה א׳ ניו יורק" and Ahuza Street "still the main street".** True (he.wiki), but not needed on the page.
- **Population, municipal area, Beit Ha'am, the planned southern neighbourhood and "רעננה מערב".** True or planned, but not about leaks.
- **Farmland flooding and a drainage plan.** Undated, and not about homes.
- **The mayor's "TAMA 38 only as demolish-and-rebuild" statement (Israel Hayom).** The page returned 403. The plan sources cover the same point.
